# Ranel — Pilot Product Catalog

## Current Phase 2 catalog — 2026-10-02

Founder-defined three-level Barber offer. **Concrete pilot scope, not evidence of completed delivery or validated demand.** Starter/Growth are manual pilot proposals; System is a concept definition, not an available app. Start with one relevant problem, not an automatic upgrade path. Public catalog: `/barber#rencana-kit`; canonical topics: `starter`, `growth`, `system`.

### Ranel Barber Starter
- **Target:** independent barber / small operator needing a documented daily foundation without complicated software.
- **Problem to discuss:** undocumented service/menu/pricing choices, inconsistent opening/closing or customer handling; do not assume every operator has these problems.
- **Included:** basic operational/customer-handling SOP; service/menu and pricing organization; daily opening/closing checklists; permission-based basic repeat-customer workflow.
- **Receives:** PDF guide plus editable document templates for SOP, menu/prices, checklists and follow-up basics. No ready-download file is claimed; pilot materials are prepared after scope agreement.
- **How:** understand current routines → agree scope → prepare documents → try one routine → review what was actually used.
- **Use case:** apply an opening/closing checklist and consistent service/menu information in daily work.
- **Status:** Pilot — discuss scope. Not a fixed-price off-the-shelf product.
- **Excludes:** POS, booking, central database, automated messages and guaranteed results.
- **Inquiry:** `/contact?offer=starter` → existing `/inquiry?offer=starter` manual WhatsApp handoff.

### Ranel Barber Growth
- **Target:** operating barber business needing clearer visit/follow-up and service/revenue review.
- **Problem to discuss:** records or follow-up are fragmented and hard to review; ask about actual behavior first.
- **Included:** all Starter foundation; customer/visit tracking structure; opt-in repeat/retention/promo workflow; basic reporting; operational review routine and small growth actions.
- **Receives:** Starter package plus editable spreadsheet templates for records/review and a manual follow-up/promo experiment guide. Customer records remain under the operator's control; no records are collected by the website.
- **How:** establish foundation → choose necessary records → try consent-based follow-up → review and choose one next action.
- **Use case:** weekly review of visits/services and one small improvement experiment.
- **Status:** Pilot — discuss scope; documents prepared to agreed needs, not a proven growth engine.
- **Excludes:** CRM, real-time analytics, loyalty engine, automated campaigns and guaranteed repeat visits/revenue.
- **Inquiry:** `/contact?offer=growth` → existing `/inquiry?offer=growth`.

### Ranel Barber System
- **Target:** operator with established routines considering a more structured digital system.
- **Problem to discuss:** several connected digital workflows need prioritization before investment.
- **Concept areas:** operational dashboard/reporting, inquiry/booking flow, customer database/history, loyalty and automation. These are **not active features**.
- **Receives in the defined concept discussion:** requirements/operational-flow document, capability priorities and scope boundaries; **not a dashboard, app access, customer database, demo, or engineering implementation**.
- **How:** discuss routines and evidence → map flows → prioritize → decide whether to defer or separately approve a narrow build.
- **Use case:** determine whether booking/history/reporting is necessary before authorizing development.
- **Status:** Concept — unavailable as software. No delivery date/engineering budget promised.
- **Excludes:** implementation of every listed digital capability, integrations, authentication, payments and automation.
- **Inquiry:** `/contact?offer=system` → `/inquiry?offer=system`, with a draft explicitly acknowledging the app is not available.

### Pilot commercial and delivery boundary

[Pricing principles](../business/pricing-and-unit-economics.md) establish **no approved price**. Display **“Harga pilot — diskusikan kebutuhan”**; no permanent amounts, subscription or fabricated discount. Before payment/work, privately agree a written scope: deliverables/formats, exclusions, inputs, price, timing, support window, acceptance/revisions and cancellation/refund handling. Do not charge for an unavailable app or accept work the founder cannot deliver. Existing [paid pilot SOP](../verticals/barber/pilot-sop.md) remains the pre-acceptance gate.

Content/version policy: catalog scope dated above; changes must be recorded explicitly. A pilot's version/revisions are specified in its agreement, not implied as perpetual support. Materials are manually prepared/delivered after agreement; this phase does not create customer-ready kit files, checkout, payments or automatic fulfillment.

Validation method: use the [blank demand-evidence template](../templates/demand-evidence.md) and [Phase 2 implementation notes](../implementation/phase-2/implementation-notes.md). No real inquiries or sales have been recorded by this implementation. Website readiness is not product-market fit.

Compatibility: old `operations`, `retention`, `tracking` links keep their Phase 1B names/messages and do not silently imply a new tier. The visible catalog uses only Starter/Growth/System. Shared source data is in `src/inquiry.ts`; public pages and inquiry drafts use it.
