# Ranel — Visual Identity Direction

**Status:** Proposed creative direction, not completed production artwork.

## Creative idea
**Order without rigidity.** Structured and dependable, but warm and approachable to independent operators.

## Wordmark
Use lowercase “ranel” in a clean humanist or restrained geometric sans-serif, medium weight, and clear letter spacing. Avoid ultra-light luxury styling, aggressive tech typography, and decorative letterforms that reduce readability.

Do not use scissors, barber poles, clippers, AI sparkles, or generic circuit-board symbols at master-brand level.

## Proposed palette
- Charcoal: #202224
- Chalk: #F2F0EB
- Olive: #5F6B4E
- Copper: #A8643A
- Stone: #8C8A84

Use color with restraint; accessibility and contrast take priority.

## Graphic language
Modular grids, clear alignment, small labels, consistent spacing, thin rules, and simple geometry. Prefer useful diagrams and annotated workflows over generic stock art.

## Photography
Show real operators and actual work settings. Keep images natural and honest. Obtain permission for identifiable people and premises; never imply endorsement without consent.

## Acceptance checks
- Legible in one color and at avatar scale.
- Works on light and dark backgrounds.
- Readable on A4 print and small mobile screens.
- Works beyond the barber vertical.
- Does not closely resemble a known third-party logo.

Before production, create several wordmark options, check font licenses, test contrast, review similarity, and obtain founder approval.
