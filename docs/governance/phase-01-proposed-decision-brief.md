# Ranel — Phase 01 Proposed Decision Brief

**Status: RECOMMENDED FOR FOUNDER REVIEW**

**Purpose:** convert the open D01–D08 questions into a concrete operating proposal so Phase 01 does not stall on ordinary ambiguity.

This document is a **recommendation**, not founder approval. The founder remains the final authority.

## 1. Executive decision

Recommended first commercial path:

> **Ranel Barber Starter** — a small, practical, barber-specific digital operating kit, delivered as editable files + PDF with bounded manual preparation and limited support.

The first version should optimize for **low friction, clear scope, low production cost, and fast market learning**, not maximum feature count.

Recommended initial commercial posture:

- first buyer: existing independent barber owner/operator or very small barber team;
- first vertical: barber;
- first product: Starter;
- transaction: one-time purchase;
- currency: IDR;
- initial payment: simple verified payment flow once the commerce/payment gates are ready;
- delivery: manual-prepared digital files at first;
- support: bounded WhatsApp support;
- license: internal use for the purchasing business; no resale/redistribution;
- price: **recommended launch candidate Rp49.000**, with **Rp79.000** as the normal-price candidate after initial validation;
- no revenue guarantee;
- no subscription;
- no custom software;
- no unlimited consulting.

The price is intentionally a recommendation, not a Phase 02 lock.

## 2. Why Starter

The existing catalog already describes Starter as the smallest candidate scope. Keeping the first product small reduces:

- production time;
- delivery complexity;
- support burden;
- refund surface;
- expectation mismatch;
- dependency on software infrastructure.

The first commercial learning question is not whether Ranel can build a complete barber operating system. It is whether a barber operator will pay for a clearly packaged improvement to a concrete operating problem.

## 3. D01 — First buyer

**RECOMMENDATION: DECIDED FOR PLANNING / FOUNDER REVIEW FOR FINAL LOCK**

First buyer:

> Independent barber owner/operator or very small barber team that already operates a shop and wants simple operating routines to become clearer and more consistent.

Priority is an **existing operating business**, not a person who has only an idea for a future barber shop.

Reason:

- existing operators can immediately use the kit;
- they can compare before/after workflow;
- the product does not need to solve pre-launch business planning;
- feedback can be based on actual use rather than hypothetical intent.

Initial market:

> Indonesia, Indonesian-language digital delivery.

This is a go-to-market choice, **not a claim that Indonesian barber demand has been validated**.

## 4. D02 — First offer

**RECOMMENDATION: Ranel Barber Starter**

Positioning:

> Practical operating kit for a small barber business.

Recommended contents:

1. Barber daily operating SOP.
2. Opening checklist.
3. Closing checklist.
4. Service/menu and price-list template.
5. Basic customer-handling routine.
6. Permission-based customer-return follow-up guide.
7. Short implementation guide.

Do not include:

- POS;
- booking system;
- CRM;
- loyalty automation;
- AI agent;
- live dashboard;
- database;
- integrations;
- custom app;
- unlimited consulting.

Delivery should be **bounded manual preparation**, not an unqualified promise of instant download.

## 5. D03 — Delivery definition

Recommended customer flow:

```
Inquiry
→ scope confirmation
→ order
→ verified payment
→ minimum business inputs
→ kit preparation
→ quality check
→ delivery
→ customer use
→ bounded support
→ feedback
```

Recommended input:

- business/shop name;
- service list;
- current price list;
- basic operating preferences;
- optional existing SOP/checklist if available.

Recommended preparation promise:

> Delivered after the agreed inputs are received and the kit passes a basic quality check.

Do **not** publish a 24-hour guarantee until actual fulfillment capacity has been measured.

Recommended delivery channel for the pilot:

- email/file delivery or another simple controlled digital delivery method;
- WhatsApp remains the support/contact layer.

## 6. D04 — Payment and price

### Payment model

**Recommendation:** one-time, upfront payment.

Reason:

- easiest commercial model for a bounded digital kit;
- no recurring entitlement complexity;
- simple customer expectation;
- aligns with low initial infrastructure;
- easier reconciliation.

### Price research

Current public Indonesian digital-product listings show a wide range:

- a basic barbershop SOP template is listed at **Rp15.000** on Digitora;
- a broad barbershop SOP/document package is listed at **Rp120.000** on LYNK/Task Flow;
- other broad SOP products are listed around **Rp70.000–Rp199.000+**, depending on scope. These are competitor asking prices, not verified sales volume or profitability. 

Sources reviewed:

- Digitora — Template SOP Barbershop: Rp15.000.
- Task Flow Indonesia — SOP & Dokumen untuk Barbershop: Rp120.000.
- Digitalesia — SOP Barbershop: Rp199.000.
- Other Indonesian SOP marketplaces show many products around Rp70.000–Rp199.000+.

### Recommended Ranel price

**Launch candidate: Rp49.000**

**Normal-price candidate: Rp79.000**

Rationale:

- above ultra-cheap generic single-template territory;
- below broad “complete SOP system” packages;
- consistent with Starter being a focused product rather than a 50–100-file library;
- leaves room for a future Growth/System ladder;
- low enough to test willingness to pay without requiring a high-trust enterprise purchase.

This is **not validated willingness-to-pay**. Phase 02 should lock one actual payable price after the founder reviews this recommendation.

### Pricing rule

Do not use fake “discount from RpX to RpY” pricing without a genuine prior price or approved promotion basis.

## 7. D05 — Cancellation

**Recommendation: simple stage-based cancellation policy.**

Before work/preparation begins:
- cancellation permitted;
- order/payment handling follows the approved payment process.

After preparation begins:
- cancellation becomes subject to the agreed scope and work already performed.

After delivery:
- ordinary change-of-mind cancellation should not automatically imply refund; defect/non-delivery/wrong-scope cases should be handled separately.

Any mandatory consumer rights that apply must be respected. Final wording should be reviewed against applicable Indonesian requirements before publication.

## 8. D06 — Refund

**Recommendation: refund/remedy for objective fulfillment problems, not guaranteed business results.**

Recommended eligible cases to define:

- payment captured but order cannot be fulfilled;
- agreed files are materially missing or unusable and cannot reasonably be corrected;
- material mismatch with the agreed scope;
- duplicate payment.

Recommended non-guaranteed cases:

- customer did not use the kit;
- customer expected revenue growth;
- customer expected more consulting than included;
- customer changed business direction after receiving the agreed product.

The final policy should distinguish **defect correction**, **replacement**, **refund**, and **business-result expectations**.

## 9. D07 — Support and fulfillment

**Recommendation: founder-led initially, with bounded support.**

Channel:

- WhatsApp/contact channel already present in the public product flow.

Support scope:

- help understanding included files;
- help with basic use;
- correction of genuine file defects;
- clarification of agreed scope.

Not included:

- unlimited consulting;
- custom software;
- ongoing business management;
- unlimited revisions;
- implementation performed indefinitely for the customer.

No hard response-time SLA should be advertised until actual operating capacity is measured.

## 10. D08 — Ownership, license, revisions

**Recommendation: internal business-use license.**

Customer may:

- use the files for the purchasing business;
- edit them for that business;
- print/use internal copies as needed.

Customer may not:

- resell the original kit;
- redistribute the original files publicly;
- package the files into another commercial product.

Recommended revision policy:

- correction of genuine product defects included;
- substantive customization is a separate service unless explicitly included;
- future updates are not automatically lifetime unless explicitly promised.

Final legal wording should be reviewed before publication.

## 11. Product ladder after validation

Do not build all tiers now.

Recommended future ladder:

### Starter — Rp49k launch candidate / Rp79k normal candidate
Focused operating kit.

### Growth — later
Starter + tracking/review tools + stronger retention/operational workflow.

### System — later
Actual software/workflow product only after recurring demand and workflow evidence exist.

### Supply — later
Physical/tools/commerce layer after product and demand evidence.

This keeps **Kits → Systems → Supply** intact without prematurely building Systems.

## 12. Validation plan

The first product should be treated as a market experiment.

Track:

- inquiries;
- qualified inquiries;
- offers sent;
- purchases;
- payment completion;
- delivery completion;
- support requests;
- refund/defect requests;
- customer usage;
- explicit feedback;
- repeat interest;
- objections to price/scope.

Do not convert a small number of sales into a broad market claim.
