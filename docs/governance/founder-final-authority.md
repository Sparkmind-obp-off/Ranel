# Ranel — Founder Final Authority Definition

**Role: Human Final Authority**

## Purpose

The founder owns final authority over Ranel while delegating ordinary decision-making to the strategy and execution layers.

## Founder is responsible for

- approving or rejecting material business direction;
- approving final commercial commitments before they become locked;
- approving material pricing/product choices when required by the roadmap gate;
- resolving strategic disagreements that cannot be resolved from evidence and constraints;
- overriding AI recommendations when they conflict with founder intent;
- accepting or rejecting major risk, legal, financial, reputational, or irreversible decisions.

## Founder is not required to

- choose every file format;
- decide every UI detail;
- choose implementation patterns;
- write technical specifications that AI can safely derive;
- answer every open decision individually;
- repeatedly unblock ordinary ambiguity.

## Review model

The preferred interaction is:

**AI prepares → AI decides where appropriate → AI executes where authorized → Founder reviews → Founder approves/changes → system continues.**

The founder may simply respond with:

- **APPROVE**
- **APPROVE WITH CHANGES**
- **REJECT**
- **HOLD FOR MORE EVIDENCE**

## Material-decision threshold

Founder review is appropriate when a decision can materially change:

- business identity or strategic direction;
- final commercial commitments;
- significant financial exposure;
- legal/regulatory obligations;
- security or privacy exposure;
- production financial truth;
- irreversible external commitments;
- brand/reputation in a way that cannot be easily reversed.

The threshold is intentionally narrower than “anything uncertain.”

## Override rule

An explicit founder instruction overrides an AI recommendation within applicable safety and platform constraints.

## Non-blocking principle

Founder authority does not mean founder must continuously operate the project. Delegated decisions should continue automatically unless a material founder-review threshold is reached.

**Core principle:**

> The founder owns the destination and final authority. The AI layers own the work required to get there.
