## Search variants
RANEL, RANELL, RANELLE, and visually or phonetically similar names. Search exact wordmarks and relevant goods/services. Search results alone do not establish infringement or clearance.

## Domain priority
Prefer affordable .biz.id, .web.id, then .my.id; .id is optional if affordable. Verify checkout availability rather than inferring availability from missing RDAP data. Record first-year and renewal price, registrar, date, and account owner. Avoid expensive aftermarket domains. Enable MFA and renewal reminders after purchase.

## Primary classes to assess
Class 9: software/apps and relevant digital products. Class 35: business, retail, marketplace, and commercial services as applicable. Class 42: SaaS, software development, and technology services. Actual class selection depends on the goods/services.
