# Ranel — Operating Model

Keep the company founder-led and lightweight until work justifies more roles or tools.

## Weekly responsibilities
- Demand: identify prospects, hold conversations, record objections.
- Sales: send clear offers, follow up with permission, record outcomes.
- Delivery: fulfill agreed scope and document exceptions.
- Product: improve the smallest workflow causing friction.
- Finance: reconcile collected cash, fees, refunds, and costs.
- Governance: review data handling, claims, access, and legal checks.

## Single source of truth
Maintain a small set of controlled records: opportunity/customer tracker, product/version register, pilot log, revenue/cost ledger, decision log, and risk list. Avoid duplicating facts without an owner.

## Customer lifecycle
Prospect identified lawfully → need qualified → offer sent → payment/scope confirmed → delivery → outcome review → support/renewal → data retained or deleted according to purpose and policy.

## Service boundaries
For every offer, define response times, support channel, included revisions, excluded work, refund/cancellation terms, and escalation path. Avoid implying unlimited support.

## Quality control
Before delivery, compare against the offer page, test files and links, check calculations with sample inputs, remove private data from demos, and provide instructions and a version number.

## Incident handling
If data is exposed, payment is mishandled, or a material error is found: contain the issue, preserve relevant facts securely, notify affected parties when appropriate, fix and verify, then record cause and prevention.
