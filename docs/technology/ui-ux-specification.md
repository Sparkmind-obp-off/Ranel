# UI/UX Specification

## Experience principles
Practical and calm; familiar Indonesian language for the initial market; mobile-first; transparent price/scope/delivery; progressive disclosure; honest status labels.

## Visual direction
Follow the brand identity document. Use restrained colors, consistent spacing, clear typography, and reusable components. Avoid generic AI imagery, fake analytics, excessive gradients, and unsupported luxury cues.

## Landing page hierarchy
1. Clear headline and audience.
2. Supporting sentence describing the offer.
3. One primary action.
4. Specific offer and intended audience.
5. Delivery process and inclusions.
6. Real, permissioned evidence only.
7. FAQs and limitations.
8. Contact and privacy information.

## Form usability
Ask only what is needed; label fields; show required/optional status; preserve values after recoverable errors; explain next steps; provide success/failure states; never pre-check marketing consent.

## Future dashboard
If validated, prioritize today's tasks, customer/pilot status, actionable metrics, and next steps. Provide mobile-friendly alternatives to wide tables. Empty states explain how to begin.

Test with 3–5 target operators when feasible. Formative usability feedback is not proof of market demand.
