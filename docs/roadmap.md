# Ranel — 90-Day Roadmap

This is a plan, not a claim that work is complete. Days begin when the founder starts.

## Days 1–14: Foundation and discovery
Complete an offer one-pager, interview 10–15 operators, document repeated problems, and complete brand/domain/trademark checks before public commercial rollout.

**Gate:** One problem repeats across independent operators and a few agree to a concrete next step.

## Days 15–30: Paid offer test
Create one minimum useful kit or fixed-scope setup service. Send clear offers to qualified operators and ask for paid pilots.

**Gate:** Payment or deposit is a meaningful signal; positive feedback alone is not. If nobody pays, investigate objections before building software.

## Days 31–60: Deliver and measure
Deliver manually to a small group. Record baseline, usage, delivery time, support burden, and feedback. Fix confusing instructions.

**Gate:** Continue if customers use the deliverable and economics look viable. Revise or stop if usage or willingness to pay is weak.

## Days 61–90: Standardize and decide
Standardize delivery, version documentation, define support boundaries, test repeat purchase where appropriate, and decide whether a lightweight system is justified.

**Gate:** Build custom software only if the workflow repeats, customers need it, and manual/no-code delivery reveals clear requirements.

## Weekly rhythm
Monday: pipeline and top constraint. Midweek: customer conversations and delivery. Friday: review cash, funnel, product use, support time, and next decision.

## Expected outputs
Evidence-backed segment, one clear paid offer, interview/objection log, pilot results and delivery economics, continue/revise/stop decision, and demand-based product backlog.
