# Ranel — Phase 01 Market & Commerce Research

Tanggal: 2026-10-03
Status: RESEARCH COMPLETE — informs decisions, does not constitute founder approval or market validation.

## 1. Purpose

This memo supplements Phase 01 business-lock work with fresh external evidence on Indonesian barber-business products, digital SOP pricing, and electronic-commerce obligations relevant to a future paid Ranel digital product.

It does **not** prove demand, sales volume, willingness to pay, profitability, legal clearance, or provider readiness.

## 2. Market/product observations

### 2.1 Low-price SOP/template segment exists

Digitora currently lists a "Template SOP Barbershop" at Rp15.000 and describes it as an editable template for owner/operators, with instant digital download. This is listing evidence only; it does not disclose sales volume or conversion. Source: https://digitora.id/chocopands/template-sop-barbershop

Digitalesia currently lists "SOP Barbershop" at Rp199.000 (shown from Rp299.000), again as a product listing rather than evidence of realized sales or willingness to pay. Source: https://digitalesia.my.id/

### 2.2 Software substitutes are materially broader and recurring

SobatBarber currently advertises packages beginning at Rp99.000/month and includes operational software such as queue/booking, POS, CRM, commissions, reports and related features. Kasera similarly advertises salon/barbershop software from Rp99.000/month with POS, booking, commissions, QRIS and reporting. These are important substitutes but are not direct price comps for a bounded digital operating kit.

Sources:
- https://sobaterp.com/barber
- https://www.kasera.id/fitur/pos

### 2.3 Implication for Ranel

Ranel Barber Starter should remain deliberately narrower than a SaaS/POS product and more useful than a generic one-file template. The working value proposition is a bounded operating kit with practical structure, editable assets, and limited preparation—not software access.

The existing candidate launch price of Rp49.000 and normal price of Rp79.000 remains a **pricing hypothesis**, not a validated price. It sits above the observed Rp15.000 template listing and below the observed Rp99.000/month software entry points and Rp199.000 SOP listing. This positioning is a strategic test hypothesis only.

## 3. Commerce/legal implications to carry into Phase 02

Indonesia's PP 71/2019 states that electronic contracts should contain, among other things, party identity, object/specification, transaction requirements, price/costs, cancellation procedure, remedies for non-conforming goods/services or hidden defects, and governing-law choice. It also requires electronic sellers to provide complete/correct information about contract terms, producer and offered product, and clarity of offer/advertising.

Source: JDIH Kemkomdigi, PP 71/2019: https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/695/t/peraturan%20pemerintah%20nomor%2071%20tahun%202019?search=by+transaksi

The official private-PSE registration guidance currently lists portals/sites/apps used to offer or trade goods/services, provide financial transaction services, and deliver paid digital content through a network among the criteria for private PSE registration. This means Phase 02/03 must include an explicit legal/compliance applicability check before Ranel turns its public site into an automated paid digital-delivery/transaction system.

Source: PSE Komdigi, criteria: https://pse.komdigi.go.id/panduan/kriteria-pendaftaran-pse-lingkup-privat

This memo does not conclude that Ranel is legally required to register in every possible operating configuration. The exact obligation depends on the final service architecture, business entity/status and applicable rules; obtain a current compliance review before automated paid launch.

## 4. Decision impact

### D01 — First buyer
Keep the recommended focus on an existing independent barber owner/operator or very small team. No evidence found here justifies a narrower city, revenue band, team size, or budget threshold.

### D02 — First offer
Keep Ranel Barber Starter as the smallest coherent candidate. Do not expand it into POS, booking, CRM, loyalty, analytics, or automation.

### D03 — Delivery
Prefer a bounded manual-prepared digital kit for the first pilot. Do not promise instant download until the files, delivery mechanism, entitlement and support path actually exist.

### D04 — Pricing/payment
Keep Rp49.000 launch / Rp79.000 normal as a test hypothesis only. Before price lock, calculate provider/payment costs, delivery labor, refund exposure and minimum viable margin. Do not describe the price as market validated.

### D05–D08 — Terms/support/licence
Make these explicit before paid automated launch: scope, delivery/acceptance, cancellation, refund/remedy, support boundaries, licence/redistribution, revision/update policy and customer-data handling.

## 5. Research limitations

- Listing prices are not transaction-volume evidence.
- No customer interviews or paid pilot were created by this research.
- Competitor feature/pricing pages can change.
- Legal sources describe rules and registration criteria; they do not replace a case-specific compliance review.
- No provider credential, payment method, merchant activation or financial readiness was verified here.
