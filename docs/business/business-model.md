# Ranel — Business Model

**Status:** LOCKED business model; activation and pricing remain evidence-gated.

## Canonical model

See [Ranel Master Blueprint](../foundation/ranel-master-blueprint.md) and [Business Architecture Lock](../foundation/ranel-business-architecture.md). This file retains the detailed business-model rationale.

## Thesis
Build a low-capital business by solving narrow operational problems for small operators. Use digital products and manual services to learn before investing in custom software or inventory.

## Three layers

### Kits
Downloadable or printable SOPs, checklists, calculators, customer-retention templates, pricing worksheets, and starter playbooks. Low marginal cost; must be specific and actionable.

### Systems
Lightweight tools or services for customer tracking, requests/appointments, reporting, loyalty, reminders, and workflow. Start manually or with no-code where practical. Build software only after recurring usage and willingness to pay are evidenced.

### Supply
Curated tools and products relevant to the vertical. Start with affiliate/referral links or a small partner model. Avoid inventory until demand, margins, returns, and fulfilment are understood. Disclose affiliate relationships.

## Initial wedge
One validated barber-operator problem. Possible areas include customer follow-up, weekly performance visibility, service SOPs, or fragmented customer notes. These are hypotheses, not confirmed demand.

## Potential revenue
1. One-time digital kit sales.
2. Paid setup or implementation.
3. Recurring fee only where ongoing value exists.
4. Training/onboarding.
5. Affiliate or distribution margin.
6. Private-label supply later, after repeat demand and compliance review.

## Cost discipline
Avoid paid APIs, custom integrations, inventory, long contracts, and broad paid ads before justified. Track fees, taxes, refunds, hosting, fulfilment, support time, and founder hours.

## Validation gate
Before substantial build time, establish a repeated problem, defined buyer, existing workaround, willingness to pay, and feasible delivery method. Likes and compliments are not purchase validation.
