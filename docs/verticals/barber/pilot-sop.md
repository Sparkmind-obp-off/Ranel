# Ranel Barber — Paid Pilot SOP

## Purpose
Deliver a small, defined result to a limited number of operators, learn from real usage, and decide whether to standardize the offer.

## Before accepting
- [ ] Confirm the operator's problem and desired outcome.
- [ ] Confirm the buyer and decision-maker.
- [ ] Agree on deliverables, exclusions, timeline, price, and support.
- [ ] Record a baseline using minimal useful data.
- [ ] Explain what data will be collected and why.
- [ ] Obtain permission for follow-up messages and case-study use.
- [ ] Do not promise revenue increases.

## Setup
1. Create an internal pilot ID.
2. Gather only required business information.
3. Configure the template or workflow.
4. Walk the operator through the first real use.
5. Confirm they can complete the core task, or record support required.

## Weekly check-in
Ask what they actually used, what was confusing, what they stopped using and why, whether the workflow replaced an existing workaround, what result is observable, and whether they would continue paying.

## Closeout
Summarize what was delivered, what was used, observed result versus baseline, limitations and confounders, customer feedback with permission, and next recommendation.

## Decision
- Continue: recurring use and payment evidence support the offer.
- Revise: problem is real but scope, workflow, or price is wrong.
- Stop: no meaningful use, no willingness to pay, or delivery economics do not work.

Never turn one pilot into a broad market claim.
