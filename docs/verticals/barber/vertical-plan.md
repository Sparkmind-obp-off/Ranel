# Ranel Barber — Vertical Plan

## Role
Barber is Ranel's first learning and revenue vertical, not the permanent limit of the master brand.

## Customer hypothesis
Independent barbershops and small teams with regular walk-in traffic and informal records. Validate segment, problem, and ability to pay.

## Workflow to investigate
Opening/preparation; walk-in handling; consultation; service delivery; payment and daily tally; customer notes and consent; follow-up; closing/stock review; weekly business review.

## Opportunity areas
- Operational checklists and SOPs.
- Customer-return and consent-based reminder workflow.
- Daily tally and weekly review sheet.
- Service/pricing and margin calculator.
- Curated supply guides.

## MVP boundary
Start with one problem and a small paid deliverable. A spreadsheet or manual service is acceptable for a pilot. Do not build a full POS, marketplace, booking engine, payroll, or multi-tenant SaaS until demand justifies it.

## Success measures
Set a baseline before the pilot: setup completion, weekly active use, workflow completion, time spent, follow-up completion, repeat purchase, and willingness to pay again. Do not claim revenue increases without a defensible measurement method.

## Boundaries
Do not import private customer data without authorization or imply endorsement from a shop where the founder works. Use synthetic demo data and obtain permission before publishing customer details or results.
