# Ranel — Go-to-Market Plan

## Objective
Find a repeatable path to first paid customers without large ad spend or premature software development.

## Stage 1 — Discovery
Talk to 10–15 reachable barber operators about recent problems and current workarounds. Do not lead with “Would you use my app?” or scrape contact lists.

## Stage 2 — Offer test
Choose one problem supported by repeated evidence. Create a one-page offer stating audience, deliverables, exclusions, price, timeline, and support. Demonstrate with synthetic data and ask for a paid pilot.

## Stage 3 — Manual delivery
Deliver personally. Record setup/support time, completion, usage, and objections. Ask permission before marketing follow-ups.

## Stage 4 — Proof and referrals
Request feedback after delivery. Publish testimonials or case studies only with permission and approval of the exact quote. Do not fabricate results.

## Channels to test
1. Founder-led conversations.
2. Relevant local business communities.
3. Short educational content showing practical workflows.
4. A simple product page or checkout.
5. Marketplaces once the offer is supportable.
6. Supplier/educator partnerships after validation.

## Content pillars
Repeatable routines, common operational blind spots, template demonstrations, honest workflow improvements, and product lessons with customer permission.

## Funnel
Track leads reached → meaningful conversations → qualified leads → offers → paid pilots → completed pilots → repeat payments/referrals. Do not optimize only for views or followers.

Pause channels that consume time without qualified conversations after a pre-agreed test.
