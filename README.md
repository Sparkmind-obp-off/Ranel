# Ranel

**Ranel** is the founder-selected master brand for a practical business-systems and commerce company serving local operators.

> **Working brand line:** Practical systems for better-run businesses.
>
> **Brand promise:** Help small operators run their businesses with more clarity, consistency, and control.

Ranel is designed to grow through three connected layers:

1. **Kits** — practical templates, SOPs, calculators, checklists, and starter playbooks.
2. **Systems** — lightweight tools for customer management, booking/requests, reporting, retention, and workflow.
3. **Supply** — relevant tools, products, and business supplies, initially through carefully selected third-party products and affiliate/distribution models.

The first vertical is **barber businesses**. Café, laundry, salon, and other local-service verticals are future options, not simultaneous launch commitments.

## Repository purpose

This repository is the **canonical documentation home for Ranel**: brand decisions, business model, product strategy, operating procedures, validation plans, and technical principles. It is not the private Bosku Cukur / Bozq One System repository. Any future codebase or customer deployment should be created and governed separately.

## Guiding principles

- Validate demand before building substantial software.
- Start with one narrow customer segment and one paid problem.
- Keep the first offer simple enough to explain and deliver manually.
- Treat kits, systems, and supply as connected layers, not three businesses to launch at once.
- Prefer low fixed costs, reversible decisions, and measurable evidence.
- Be honest about what is live, tested, integrated, and commercially validated.
- Collect only customer data needed to deliver the service; use consent-based communication.
- Never present an unverified brand, domain, integration, or business result as confirmed.

## Suggested initial sequence

**Discover → sell a small paid pilot → deliver manually → measure → standardize → automate only where justified.**
